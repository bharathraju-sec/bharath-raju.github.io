// About HEXBREACH

HEXBREACH is an independent publishing platform for offensive and defensive security research. No ads. No SEO-bait. Just technical depth from practitioners who've been in the trenches.

// Our Mission

The security industry has a knowledge-sharing problem. Too much research lives behind NDAs, conference paywalls, or gets buried in dense academic papers that practitioners never read.

HEXBREACH exists to change that. We're a community-first platform where researchers, pentesters, malware analysts, and blue teamers publish their work openly — detailed, technical, and without dumbing it down.

Every post goes through a light technical review by community moderators before publishing. We don't filter based on "controversial" findings — responsible disclosure is respected, and we have a clear policy on how to handle active vulnerabilities.

We believe that sharing attack techniques openly is the only way defenders ever catch up. Security through obscurity is not security.

// Disclosure Policy

We follow a 90-day responsible disclosure policy for unpatched vulnerabilities. Contact us before publishing if you believe your research may affect live systems at scale.

Anonymous submissions are accepted. We support Tor and I2P access.

// What We Stand For
🔓
Open Knowledge
Security knowledge hoarded is security knowledge wasted. We believe in publishing technical depth openly so defenders everywhere can benefit.
🛡️
Responsible Disclosure
We take active vulnerability disclosure seriously. Our editorial team works with authors to ensure unpatched critical vulnerabilities are handled with appropriate care.
🕵️
Anonymity Respected
Researchers who need to stay anonymous can publish without a verified identity. We don't log IPs or share author data with third parties.
📡
No Ads, Ever
HEXBREACH runs on community support and reader subscriptions. Zero advertising, zero affiliate links, zero vendor influence on editorial decisions.
⚙️
Technical Rigour
Every post is reviewed for technical accuracy before publishing. We don't publish marketing-speak or unverified claims dressed as research.
🌐
Community-First
HEXBREACH is owned by its community, not a VC-backed corporation. Decisions about the platform's direction are made with author and reader input.
